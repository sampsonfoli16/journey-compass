The process

How Journey Compass works

Four steps, about five minutes start to finish.

  1. 1

    Tell us who you are

    Fill in your name, student ID, email and phone number. Every field is checked as you type, so you will know right away if something needs fixing.

  2. 2

    Answer 8 questions

    A mix of multiple-choice, one audio scenario and one image hotspot question, all within a 240-second timer.

  3. 3

    We tally your answers

    Each answer earns points across four categories: communication, critical thinking, time management and leadership.

  4. 4

    Get your Learning Journey Plan

    See your full score breakdown, your top strength, and a concrete next step for building on it.
